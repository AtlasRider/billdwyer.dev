About this app

Versatile engineer with experience across multiple technologies, specializing in Android applications for the past 10+ years. Driven by a passion for learning and problem-solving — finding innovative ways of connecting users and technology is this developer's favorite kind of bug report.

Began as a .NET/C# generalist building insurance and CRM systems in the Midwest, then took a hard left turn to ride a motorcycle from Arizona to Argentina (20,000 miles, 8 months, one book written about it). Returned, pivoted into mobile, and has spent the last decade-plus shipping Android apps at scale — from a trucking load board to an observability platform to eBay's item discovery surfaces. Comfortable leading, mentoring, and migrating very large codebases without anyone noticing until it's done.
